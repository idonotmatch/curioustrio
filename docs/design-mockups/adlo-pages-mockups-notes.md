## Adlo OAuth Pages Mockups

These mockups are intentionally simple and trust-forward.

Design goals:
- build off the existing `hellodang.com` minimal aesthetic
- keep the pages static and lightweight
- make the app feel credible for Google OAuth review
- use a calm reading layout instead of marketing-page choreography

Shared styling direction:
- white background
- near-black primary text
- muted gray secondary text
- soft blue accent pulled from the Adlo icon family
- rounded but restrained surfaces
- centered, narrow hero on `/adlo`
- left-aligned readable column on privacy and terms pages

Recommended real URLs:
- `https://www.hellodang.com/adlo`
- `https://www.hellodang.com/adlo/privacy`
- `https://www.hellodang.com/adlo/terms`

Content assumptions used in the mockups:
- product name: Adlo
- effective date: April 5, 2026
- support email: `dang@hellodang.com`
- monetization posture: no personalized ads, no Gmail-data advertising, room for premium and affiliate/contextual recommendations
