# Adlo Investor / Partner One-Pager

## What Adlo Is

Adlo is building a mobile-first personal finance companion that helps people not only record what they spent, but understand what is changing, what is recurring, and where they can make better decisions.

Traditional finance apps are good at:

- showing balances
- categorizing transactions
- tracking budgets after the fact

Adlo is being built to go further:

- capture expenses from text, receipts, and email
- understand recurring purchase behavior at the item level
- explain monthly spending patterns in plain language
- surface proactive insights before or as financial decisions happen

The long-term vision is an adaptive "financial companion OS" that becomes more useful as it learns from a user’s actual behavior.

## Why This Matters

Most financial tools stop at reporting:

- what you spent
- whether you are over budget
- what category changed

Users still have to interpret:

- whether the change is normal
- whether it is driven by one-off purchases or ongoing habits
- whether recurring essentials are getting more expensive
- whether they should change their budget
- whether they are about to need something again and should buy more intelligently

Adlo is focused on that interpretation layer.

## Core Product Experience Today

Adlo already supports:

- manual expense capture from natural language
- receipt scan capture
- Gmail-based import of receipts and order confirmations
- personal and household budgeting
- household-aware expense sharing and review flows
- pending review queue for uncertain imports
- item extraction and normalization for purchases
- recurring purchase detection
- recurring item history
- an insights surface in the Summary tab

The product has moved beyond a basic tracker into an early intelligence product with several already-strong operational surfaces.

## What Makes Adlo Different

### 1. Capture quality
Expenses can enter the system through multiple real-world channels:

- typed input
- scanned receipts
- imported email receipts

This lowers friction and gives the product richer source data than manual-only trackers.

### 2. Item-level understanding
Adlo is being built to understand not just transactions, but what was actually bought.

That matters for:

- recurring household essentials
- price changes over time
- repurchase timing
- future recommendations and savings opportunities

### 3. Insight layer
Instead of stopping at dashboards, Adlo is building an insight engine that can explain:

- pace versus historical spending
- category drivers
- recurring cost pressure
- repurchase timing
- price variance on recurring purchases

### 4. Household + personal coexistence
The product is designed for both:

- personal financial behavior
- shared household decision-making

That creates a wider and more realistic use case than single-user budgeting alone.

## What Comes Next

The next important unlocks are:

### 1. External price observation
This enables proactive "buy now, it is cheaper" experiences for recurring purchases.

### 2. Deeper insight drill-downs
This strengthens user trust and makes the intelligence layer more actionable.

### 3. Better ranking and personalization
This is the bridge from heuristic intelligence to an adaptive system that becomes more useful over time.

### 4. Deeper household collaboration experience
The underlying household model is already real; the next step is making shared financial behavior feel more collaborative and differentiated.

## Long-Term Vision

The end state is not another budgeting app.

It is a personalized financial operating layer that can answer:

- Are you spending faster than usual?
- What is driving the change?
- Are your recurring purchases getting more expensive?
- Do you usually need this item around now?
- Is there a better time or place to buy it?
- Is your budget realistic for your actual life?

The more a user captures, the more Adlo learns, and the more useful it becomes.

That is the core thesis:

- financial value should compound with real usage
- the product should move from logging to guidance
- and from static reporting to adaptive, personal intelligence
